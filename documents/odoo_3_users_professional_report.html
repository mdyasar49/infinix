9. Comprehensive Process Flow & Lead Management Verification: Is the Business Flow Working?

This section provides a detailed, beginner-friendly verification of the entire end-to-end sales lifecycle. Both the Process Flow (overall sales governance) and the Lead Management Process (daily prospect handling) documents have been verified against the live CC_Live_Stage database. All 5 core operational phases are fully operational and working properly.

Phase 1: Lead Ingestion & Web Enquiry Capture

How it works (Simple Explanation): When a prospective client fills out an enquiry form on the Canadian Crystalline website, or submits a product query on IndiaMART / TradeIndia, the system catches the lead immediately without needing anyone to type it manually.

  • Technical Odoo Engine: The automated webhook ingests payload data and instantly creates a new lead in Stage 1: New Enquiry - HOD (Stage ID 1).
  • Channel Attribution: Lead Source tags (e.g. IndiaMART, Website Contact, Buyleads) are automatically attached, exactly matching legacy EMS marketing channels.
  • Verification & Test Result: [FIXED & OPERATIONAL] Test leads submitted via external form create valid crm.lead records with complete customer name, email, phone, and requirements.

Phase 2: HOD Review & Sales Team Lead Allotment

How it works (Simple Explanation): The Head of Department (HOD) reviews incoming leads to determine the project type (e.g. RO Plant, Bottling Line, Effluent Treatment) and assigns the lead to the most qualified sales executive.

  • Technical Odoo Engine: HOD opens the dedicated HOD Dashboard (Action 814) or views Stage 1 cards. Clicking Auto Assign or Notify Team assigns the Salesperson (user_id) and Sales Team (team_id).
  • Automated Stage Transition: As soon as allotment occurs, the lead automatically advances from Stage 1 to Stage 2: Allotted (Stage ID 2).
  • Notification System: The assigned salesperson receives an internal activity notice and an email alert containing the lead details.
  • Verification & Test Result: [FIXED & OPERATIONAL] Allotment logic successfully binds salespeople and moves leads into Stage 2.

Phase 3: Sales Rep Follow-Up & Daily Sales Activity Logging

How it works (Simple Explanation): The assigned sales representative contacts the client daily by phone, email, or meeting. Every conversation must be logged so that managers know the exact status and callback schedule.

  • Technical Odoo Engine: Sales reps and CC Admin use the Daily Sales Activity register (Action 863) to log follow-up notes, next action dates, and call summaries.
  • Management Summary Dispatch: Automated daily email reporting (Action 864) collects all daily sales logs and emails an executive summary to department heads every evening.
  • Kanban Pipeline Progression: Depending on client response, the card moves through intermediate stages: Callback (Stage 3), Technical Discussion, Client Visit, up to Details Sent.
  • Verification & Test Result: [FIXED & OPERATIONAL] Daily sales activities are correctly logged in chatter and displayed in the management register.

Phase 4: Quotation Generation & Multi-Tier Approval Workflow

How it works (Simple Explanation): Because water treatment machinery involves high capital expenditure, sales reps cannot send discounted quotes without management authorization. Quotes must be approved by senior management before reaching the customer.

  • Technical Odoo Engine: Inside the lead, the sales rep clicks New Quotation, adds equipment line items, and submits the quote.
  • Multi-Level Approval Stages: If approval is required, the lead moves to Stage 18: Quotation Approval Requested. Authorized managers view the quotation and can click Approve or Reject.
  • Status Branching: Approval moves the lead to Stage 19: Quotation Approved, allowing the PDF quote to be dispatched to the customer. If rejected, it moves to Stage 20: Quotation Rejected with mandatory feedback notes.
  • Verification & Test Result: [FIXED & OPERATIONAL] Multi-tier approval gate prevents unauthorized quote dispatches and enforces management sign-off.

Phase 5: Deal Won, Order Confirmation & Delivery Handover

How it works (Simple Explanation): The client accepts the quotation and issues an official Purchase Order. The deal is marked as "WON". The software automatically notifies manufacturing and dispatch teams to build and ship the equipment.

  • Technical Odoo Engine: The quotation is confirmed into a confirmed Sales Order (sale.order). The CRM lead automatically moves to Stage 21: Order Finalized / Won (Stage ID 21).
  • Downstream Operations: The confirmation automatically creates inventory delivery pickings (stock.picking) and links execution projects (project.project).
  • Smart Stat Shortcuts: Administrator can immediately jump into delivery shipments, manufacturing orders, and project task boards directly from the quotation header.
  • Verification & Test Result: [FIXED & OPERATIONAL] Won status correctly generates downstream operational orders without manual re-entry.
